## Creature (Character and Monster) stats

There are 8 total stats, divided into three main categories:

ATK stats – giving strengths of different attacks:
  - **STR** (Strength) – *physical (melee) damage*
  - **INT** (Intelligence) – *magical damage*
    
    Magical attacks can be of types:
    - Fixed-target.
      Casted directly on creatures, chasing them down.
    - Cell-target.
      Targets creatures, but is casted on the cell.
      (i.e. lightning bolt)
    - Area-target.
      Casted on land area, affecting creatures in its radius.

  - **DEX** (Dexterity) – *physical (range) damage*

DEF stats – providing defences from different attacks:
  - **CON** (Constitution) – *physical (melee) defense*
  - **WIS** (Wisdom) – *magical defense*
  - **AGI** (Agility) – *physical (range) attacks protection*
    
    Range attacks can be dodged completely with some chance depending on AGI,
    or partially (reducing the damage).

    Also gives chance to dodge *magical* (non fixed-target) attacks
    by moving character to the adjascent cell in the case.


EXTRA stats – for lulz and adding interesting mechanics:
  - **LUK** (Luck)
  - **INS** (Insanity)
